PUBLIC EXPERIMENTAL PROTOCOL

Rules of the experiment.

Designed to make the experiment informative even if the model loses money.

01 / CAPITAL

Equal starting budgets

$1,000 stocks and $1,000 crypto, separate cash balances, holdings, decisions, and benchmarks. No transfers between them.

02 / INTEGRITY

Prospective only

Decision files are first recorded as pending. A trade can fill only on a later eligible market bar, never on the price that was already known when it was submitted.

03 / RISK

No leverage

Long-only spot assets, no short-selling, no borrowing, no derivatives. Trades incur simulated slippage and fees. New positions must satisfy pre-set sizing limits.

04 / BASELINE

Passive comparison

Stocks compare with SPY; crypto with BTC. Both benchmarks initialize on the first usable price observation and are marked to market alongside the portfolios.

05 / MODEL

Agent provenance

Market-data ingestion is automated separately. The site does not claim to run GPT unless an authenticated decision-writing process is operational and logged.

06 / EVIDENCE

Failures stay visible

Missing quotes, rejected orders, delayed executions and model inactivity are reported; incomplete records are never filled with fabricated prices or hindsight decisions.
