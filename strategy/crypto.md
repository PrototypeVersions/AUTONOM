# Crypto strategy

Version 1 · November 11, 2026

## Objective

Outperform BTC after simulated trading costs over a sustained forward period. Track drawdown and cash exposure alongside returns. Use a multiweek thesis for entries and check it daily.

## Decision process

- Compare BTC with cash first. Use completed Coinbase USD daily candles to check BTC against its 20-day average and the direction of that average. Use recent returns and reliable market developments for context. Stabilizing or improving conditions can support a small initial BTC position of roughly 5–15%. A weak trend, unreliable market data, or a material liquidity or venue shock can support cash.
- Consider another eligible coin when it has a distinct thesis, reliable USD pricing and sufficient liquidity. Compare it with BTC after estimated costs. The research universe is BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, LINK, AVAX, DOGE, LTC and BCH. Give thin or highly correlated alternatives a higher bar for inclusion.
- If holding cash, name the condition that would make an entry worth reconsidering, such as a completed close back above the 20-day average with improving short-term momentum. If a statistic cannot be verified from candles, identify what is missing. Record a review point and the risk of staying out.
- For a buy, explain sizing and what would break the thesis. Check existing positions and pending orders each run. Keep spot exposure within the execution rules.
